### Why Cultural Heritage Items Remain Empty

According to the baseline audit analysis, "文化传承与保护" (Cultural Heritage & Protection) is a **client-specific topic** with **no corresponding clauses** in the five source frameworks (SSE, HKEX, GRI, MSCI, CSA-COS). The audit files explicitly note:

> "文化传承/非遗/东方文化为客户特定议题，五源无直接对应条款"

This topic covers:
- 非遗 (Intangible Cultural Heritage)
- 传统工艺 (Traditional Crafts)
- 东方文化元素 (Oriental Cultural Elements)
- 海外文化传播 (Overseas Cultural Communication)

These are unique to 花西子's business focus on Oriental beauty and cultural heritage, which is not covered by standard ESG disclosure frameworks.
